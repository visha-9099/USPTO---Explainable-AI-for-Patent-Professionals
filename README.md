🏛 USPTO - Explainable AI for Patent Professionals
This project focuses on integrating Explainable AI (XAI) into the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) workflow to assist patent 
professionals in evaluating and processing patent applications efficiently. The primary goal is to develop AI models that enhance patent classification, 
prior art search, and legal reasoning, while ensuring interpretability and transparency in decision-making.

📌 Project Overview
🔹 Objective: Develop AI-driven solutions to support patent professionals in analyzing patents with transparency and explainability.
🔹 Scope: Includes patent classification, similarity detection, prior art search, and automated summarization.
🔹 Key Focus: Enhancing interpretability of AI decisions using Explainable AI (XAI) techniques.
🔹 Industry Impact: Streamlining patent examination processes, reducing manual workload, and improving decision accuracy.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
Python 🐍 – Core language for AI model development
Natural Language Processing (NLP) 🧠 – Extracting insights from patent documents
Transformers (BERT, GPT, T5) 🚀 – Advanced language models for text understanding
SHAP & LIME 📊 – Explainability techniques for model interpretation
FastAPI / Flask 🌐 – Deploying AI-based patent analysis tools
USPTO Dataset 📄 – Official patent data for training and evaluation

📊 Key Features & Methodologies
✅ Patent Classification – AI-powered tagging of patent applications into relevant technology classes.
✅ Prior Art Search – AI-assisted retrieval of similar patents to assess novelty and patentability.
✅ Explainable AI (XAI) – Providing transparency in AI decisions using attention mechanisms, SHAP, and LIME.
✅ Automated Summarization – Generating concise summaries of lengthy patent documents.
✅ Legal Language Processing – Fine-tuning AI models to understand complex legal jargon.

📈 Challenges & Findings
Patent documents are lengthy, complex, and contain domain-specific jargon, making NLP challenging.
Transparency in AI decisions is crucial for trust and adoption by patent professionals.
Balancing accuracy and interpretability is a key challenge in AI-driven legal decision-making.

🚀 Future Scope
🔹 Improving domain adaptation for AI models in patent law
🔹 Enhancing visual explanations for legal professionals
🔹 Integrating interactive AI assistants for patent examiners
